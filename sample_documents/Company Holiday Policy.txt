# COMPANY HOLIDAY POLICY - NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES

1. HOLIDAY CALENDAR & ENTITLEMENTS
Nexora Technologies observes 10 mandatory public holidays and provides 2 optional/floating holidays per calendar year.

2. MANDATORY HOLIDAYS CALENDAR (2026)
- New Year's Day: January 1
- Republic Day: January 26
- Good Friday: April 3
- International Workers' Day: May 1
- Independence Day: August 15
- Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti: October 2
- Dussehra: October 20
- Diwali: November 8
- Christmas Day: December 25
- Year-End Holiday: December 31

3. FLOATING HOLIDAYS RULE
- Employees may choose up to 2 optional holidays from the regional floating holiday list.
- Floating holiday requests must be submitted 7 days prior for manager approval.
