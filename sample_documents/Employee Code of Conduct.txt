# EMPLOYEE CODE OF CONDUCT - NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES

1. PROFESSIONAL ETHICS AND INTEGRITY
At Nexora Technologies, we maintain the highest standards of professional conduct, integrity, honesty, and mutual respect.

2. WORKPLACE DIVERSITY AND ANTI-HARASSMENT
- Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, or sexual orientation.
- All employees must promote an inclusive, collaborative, and safe work environment.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
- Proprietary source code, customer records, financial data, and AI models are strictly confidential.
- Sharing confidential information outside authorized channels or using unauthorized personal cloud storage is a severe policy violation.

4. IT RESOURCES AND DEVICE USE
- Enterprise hardware and network assets are intended primarily for official company duties.
- Personal use must be minimal and must not compromise network bandwidth or IT security protocols.
