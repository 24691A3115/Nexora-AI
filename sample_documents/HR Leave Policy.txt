# HR LEAVE POLICY - NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy outlines the leave entitlements, eligibility, and procedure for applying for leave at Nexora Technologies. All full-time employees are covered under this policy.

2. LEAVE CATEGORIES AND ENTITLEMENTS
- Casual Leave (CL): All full-time employees are entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year. Casual leave can be taken for personal matters and cannot be carried forward to the next year. Maximum consecutive CL allowed is 3 days.
- Sick Leave (SL): Employees are entitled to 10 days of Paid Sick Leave per year. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a registered medical practitioner's certificate is required.
- Annual / Earned Leave (EL): Employees accrue 15 days of Earned Leave annually after completing 1 year of continuous service. Earned leave can be accumulated up to a maximum of 30 days.
- Maternity Leave: Female employees are eligible for 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children.
- Paternity Leave: Male employees are eligible for 5 consecutive days of paid paternity leave within 6 months of child birth.

3. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCEDURE
- All leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance via the Nexora Portal or Email Assistant.
- Emergency leave or sick leave must be notified to the manager before 10:00 AM on the day of absence.
- Approvals must be granted by the direct reporting manager and HR manager.

4. UNEXCUSED ABSENCE
Absence without prior approval for more than 5 consecutive working days will be treated as voluntary abandonment of service.
